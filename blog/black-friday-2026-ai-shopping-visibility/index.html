Black Friday 2026: AI-Driven Retail Traffic Is Forecast to Grow 130%. Will AI Recommend Your Store?

By Tandeep Sangra, She Innovates AI
October 30, 2026
11 min read
TL;DR for e-commerce brands: Adobe forecasts US shoppers will spend $275.1 billion online in November and December 2026, and that AI-driven traffic to US retail sites will grow 130% year over year. Shoppers are starting to ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity what to buy before they visit any store. If AI cannot clearly understand your products, prices and brand, a rival gets named instead. Black Friday is on 27 November, so use the weeks before it to fix your product data, add structured data, answer real shopping questions, and test what AI says about you. ChatGPT Ads are a separate, paid option worth testing on a small scale.

Picture a shopper typing: "Which skincare products are worth buying for sensitive skin under $50?" Or: "Compare the best wireless headphones for travelling." Or: "Where can I find a good Black Friday deal on running shoes?"

Instead of opening a dozen tabs, they read one written answer that names a handful of products and stores. Is your brand one of them?

This guide uses the latest holiday forecast from Adobe to show how big the shift is, what is solid and what is still uncertain, and what an e-commerce team can do in the weeks before Black Friday. Thanksgiving is on 26 November, Black Friday on 27 November and Cyber Monday on 30 November 2026.

The number to watch: AI-driven traffic

One forecast deserves more attention than the rest. Adobe measured AI-driven traffic to US retail sites up 127% year over year in August 2026, and expects 130% growth across the holiday season and 141% on Thanksgiving Day.

Read it carefully. A growth rate tells you the direction and speed of travel, not the size. Adobe's figures here do not say what share of all retail visits come from AI, and search, ads and marketplaces still bring most shoppers. The sensible conclusion is not "drop everything for AI". It is "AI is becoming a real route to your store, so make sure you are ready for it."

There is also a quality signal. In August 2026, Adobe found AI-driven visitors converted 48% higher, generated 43% more revenue per visit, added to cart 32% more often and showed 16% higher engagement than other traffic.

What is changing in how shoppers find products

Holiday shoppers rarely begin with a brand name. They ask questions:

  • Which gifts are best for a 10-year-old?
  • Which laptop is worth buying during Black Friday?
  • What are the best affordable skincare products for dry skin?
  • Which travel bags offer the best value?
  • Where can I find sustainable gifts under $100?

These are discovery questions, and AI tools can answer them by explaining options, comparing products and naming brands. They are also fertile ground for e-commerce: the categories above include electronics ($63.3 billion online), apparel ($51.3 billion) and a fast-growing cosmetics category (+9.5%).

For a store, the new test is whether AI systems can understand your catalogue: your product features, prices, availability and what makes your range relevant. If they cannot, your brand may be overlooked, or described inaccurately, just when a shopper is deciding.

It is worth knowing how these answers are often built. A 2025 research preprint comparing AI search with traditional search found that AI tools relied heavily on earned media, meaning third-party and authoritative sources, and gave much less weight to brand-owned content. In plain terms, what others say about your store matters alongside what your own product pages say. (It is a preprint, so read it with its experimental design in mind.) Five things e-commerce brands can fix before Black Friday

You do not need to rebuild your marketing. Strengthen the foundations.

1 · Make product information AI-ready

Product titles, descriptions, specifications, prices, availability, images and attributes should be clear, accurate and identical wherever they appear. Outdated prices and stock status are among the first things to cause wrong answers.

2 · Add structured data

Use Product, Offer, Review and Organization markup where it applies, following Google's product structured data guidance. It helps systems interpret what you sell. It does not guarantee a mention, and Google says no special markup is required for its AI features. See our schema markup services.

3 · Answer real shopping questions

Go beyond descriptions built around product names. Say what each product is best for, who should buy it, how it compares and what to consider first. This is the heart of answer engine optimization.

4 · Build trust beyond product pages

Reviews, credible mentions, clear delivery and returns policies, and consistent business details all shape how a shopper, and an AI, judges your store. Fix old or conflicting information wherever it lives.

5 · Test your AI visibility, repeatedly

Ask your shopping questions across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI search. Check whether your brand and products appear, which competitors are named, which sources are cited, and whether the details are right. Do not rely on one prompt or one result; answers change between runs and tools. Our AI citation monitoring page explains ongoing tracking, and our free AI visibility scan gives you a first look.

Honest expectations. No one can promise that an AI tool will recommend your store, and fixes made now may not show up in every tool before Black Friday. Tools that search the web can reflect a corrected page sooner than those that rely on what they learned in training. Start now, measure, and keep going after the sale.

Another option this season: ChatGPT Ads

For the first time, ChatGPT Ads are entering the Black Friday and holiday advertising mix. That opens a paid route for e-commerce brands alongside organic AI visibility, and the two should not be confused.

Organic AI visibility

  • Earned by clear, trustworthy information
  • No guarantee, builds over time
  • Shapes what AI says about you

ChatGPT Ads

  • Paid, clearly labelled placements
  • Appear separately from ChatGPT's answers
  • OpenAI says they do not influence the answers

In traditional search advertising, targeting often starts with a keyword. In ChatGPT, people share more context about what they need and compare choices through conversation. OpenAI has expanded its advertising platform and introduced a self-serve Ads Manager, with availability that depends on country. For the mechanics and pricing, read how ChatGPT Ads work and what ChatGPT Ads cost.

Five quick wins if you want to test ChatGPT Ads this holiday season

  • →
    Start with clear buying intent. Pick products that solve a specific problem, have strong seasonal demand or suit gifting. Do not promote the whole catalogue at first.
  • →
    Build campaigns around customer needs. Think beyond "Black Friday Sale". Lead with the problem, the category and the benefit.
  • →
    Make the creative specific. Say what the product is, its strongest benefit and a relevant offer. Avoid vague promotional language.
  • →
    Match the landing page to the ad. Send shoppers to the gift collection you advertised, not your homepage, with prices, discounts, stock, shipping and returns easy to see.
  • →
    Test, measure, improve. Start with a focused campaign and a defined budget, watch impressions, clicks and conversions where measurement is available, then refine.

Remember: a new advertising channel is an opportunity to test, not a guarantee of sales.

The takeaway

Two different opportunities, one holiday plan

AI search visibility is about being understood and recommended when shoppers ask. ChatGPT Ads are about paying to appear in front of them. Both deserve a place in your holiday planning, and the first one starts with accurate product information you control today.
