What Is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO = Answer Engine Optimization. It means formatting and optimizing your content so that AI-powered search tools, voice assistants and chatbots such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews choose your brand as the direct answer or citation.

SEO aims for a position

The target is to rank a page among the ten blue links, so searchers click through to your site.

AEO aims for the answer

The target is the exact, concise answer an AI engine pulls out and reads aloud or shows on screen, with your brand credited.

AEO is narrower than GEO, which covers how AI platforms describe and recommend your brand as a whole. Many businesses need both, and our GEO services cover that side.

What We Do Under AEO

Our AEO service makes your content the clear, useful answer to the questions your audience asks. We find the questions worth winning, write answer-first content for them, structure each page so AI search systems can pull out and understand the answer, and back it with evidence. The work covers question-based topic targeting, answer-first writing, FAQ and conversational content, AI-friendly page structures, schema markup, E-E-A-T signals, original research and data, and ongoing optimisation based on how your content performs in AI search. The goal is simple: make it easy for AI systems to retrieve, interpret and use your content when they answer a relevant question.

1

Write answer-first content

  • Lead with the answer. The core answer, definition or summary comes in the first 40 to 60 words of a section, with the background after it.
  • Write the way people ask. Natural-language, long-tail questions and "People Also Ask" queries, matching how people speak to voice and AI assistants.
  • Make each section stand alone. Clear subheadings and paragraphs that still make sense when an AI lifts them out of the page.
2

Structure content for AI parsing

  • Use scannable formats. Bullets, numbered lists, comparison tables and clear H2 and H3 headings in place of dense paragraphs.
  • Add schema markup. FAQ, HowTo, Article and Product structured data, so machines can read what each page contains.
  • Keep it retrievable. Key text sits in plain, visible HTML, not behind pop-ups, slow scripts or blocked elements.
3

Build authority and trust (E-E-A-T)

  • Show your experts. Author names, credentials and update dates on the page and in schema.
  • Be present where AI looks. Brand mentions on external knowledge hubs, industry forums and channels such as Reddit, LinkedIn and YouTube.
  • Publish original data. Unique statistics, research and quotable facts that AI platforms can cite as a primary source.
4

Measure and monitor AI visibility

  • Track AI referral traffic. Visits from AI domains such as chatgpt.com and perplexity.ai show up in your analytics.
  • Use citation tracking. Dedicated tools show how often engines cite your brand. We are building our own around this, and our free AI Visibility Check gives you a starting snapshot.
  • Run manual audits. Regularly ask the major AI platforms your target questions and record whether you are recommended.

Re-test the same questions, track referrals and citations, and improve the pages that are not yet winning.
