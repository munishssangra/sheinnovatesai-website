What Is Tandeep Sangra's AI Visibility Methodology?

Traditional SEO is built around keywords: density, placement, rankings. AI Visibility doesn't work that way — ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews don't rank pages or match keyword terms; they synthesize an answer and decide, source by source, who's trustworthy enough to cite. Tandeep's methodology — applied company-wide at She Innovates AI as Citation Outcome Engineering — is built entirely around that distinction: entity clarity, structural extractability, and third-party trust signals, not keywords. The full framework is detailed on the About page.
