Our Methodology — Citation Outcome Engineering

What Is Citation Outcome Engineering?

AI Visibility isn't one task — it's an outcome. Citation Outcome Engineering is how we get there: combining technical optimization (schema, entity signals, content structure), platform-specific strategy (AEO for answer engines, GEO for generative engines), and continuous measurement — so you're tracking whether AI platforms cite you, not guessing.

Every service on this page sits under this one methodology, whether it's making your brand citable in ChatGPT's answers, or getting your ad in front of someone asking ChatGPT for a recommendation.

What LLMs Look For

What Makes a Brand "AI-Ready"?

Before AI systems can cite, recommend, or correctly represent your brand, they need to find clear signals to work from. Here's what they're actually looking for.

1

Structured, Machine-Readable Data

Schema markup that tells AI systems exactly who you are, what you offer, and how your entities connect — not left to guesswork.

2

Answer-First Content

Direct, extractable answers near the top of the page — not buried under three paragraphs of preamble.

3

Consistent Entity Signals

The same name, facts, and details about your brand everywhere — inconsistency reads as unreliable to an AI system.

4

Original Data & Evidence

Research, statistics, or first-hand insight AI systems can cite as a source — not content that just restates what's already out there.

5

Credible External Mentions

Third-party references and citations that corroborate what your own site says about you.

6

Accurate Local & NAP Data

Clean, consistent Name-Address-Phone details across directories — small mismatches create real ambiguity for AI systems.

Schema Markup & Structured Data

The machine-readable layer that tells AI exactly who you are.

  • Full JSON-LD entity structure, not just basic SEO schema
  • AI-relevant properties: sameAs, knowsAbout, worksFor
  • Validated for technical accuracy & compliance
We build the machine-readable layer of your website — structured data that helps AI search engines and LLMs clearly understand your brand, expertise, services, content, and their relationships. Our implementation goes beyond basic SEO schema to build an interconnected JSON-LD entity structure using relevant types such as Organization, Person, Service, Product, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Article, FAQ, VideoObject, and LocalBusiness. We strengthen the graph with AI-relevant properties such as sameAs, knowsAbout, worksFor, memberOf, and alumniOf where applicable — creating clearer entity connections and stronger signals for AEO, GEO, and AI Search visibility.
