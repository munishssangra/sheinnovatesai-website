Our Methodology — Citation Outcome Engineering

What Is Citation Outcome Engineering?

AI Visibility isn't one task — it's an outcome. Citation Outcome Engineering is how we get there: combining technical optimization (schema, entity signals, content structure), platform-specific strategy (AEO for answer engines, GEO for generative engines), and continuous measurement — so you're tracking whether AI platforms cite you, not guessing.

Every engagement we run, and every person on our team, works under this one methodology. It's not a philosophy we talk about — it's the system every deliverable comes out of.

We name it, define it, and apply it consistently across everything we publish — on purpose. A named methodology is something AI search engines themselves can recognize, cite, and attribute to us specifically, rather than folding us into generic "SEO agency" results.
