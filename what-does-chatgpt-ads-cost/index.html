What Does ChatGPT Ads Cost? A Business Guide Before You Spend

CPC, CPM, oCPC, oCPM, minimum budgets, and what to actually set aside for your first campaign — explained without the jargon.

TL;DR — What does ChatGPT Ads cost?

There's no fixed price. Cost depends on your campaign objective and billing method: Views is billed on CPM, Clicks is billed on CPC (with OpenAI currently recommending a $3–$5 max CPC bid), and Conversions can use oCPC or oCPM. The minimum daily budget is $25 USD (or the equivalent in other currencies), but treat that as a floor, not a target — a sensible first campaign budgets for learning, not scale.

Thinking about advertising on ChatGPT?

The first question most businesses ask is simple:

“How much does ChatGPT Ads cost?”

But that usually leads to more questions.

  • What is the minimum budget?
  • How much does a click cost?
  • Do I pay for impressions or conversions?
  • Can I control how much I spend?
  • Which campaign objective should I choose?

And perhaps the biggest question:

“How much should we actually budget for our first campaign?”

Let’s break it down.

So, what does ChatGPT Ads cost?

There isn’t one fixed price for running an ad on ChatGPT.

Your cost depends on the campaign objective, billing method, bidding, delivery and competition for relevant ad opportunities.

OpenAI currently offers three campaign objectives:

  • Views — optimized for visibility and billed on CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions)
  • Clicks — optimized for traffic and billed on CPC (cost per valid click)
  • Conversions — optimized toward a supported conversion event, with eligible advertisers able to choose oCPC or oCPM billing.

So before asking, “What does a ChatGPT ad cost?”, you need to ask:

“What are we trying to achieve?”

What is the minimum budget for ChatGPT Ads?

For accounts billed in USD, the current minimum daily campaign budget is $25/day.

The minimum varies by billing currency. For example, OpenAI currently lists minimum daily budgets of €15 for EUR accounts, £15 for GBP accounts, and CAD 25 for CAD accounts.

But the minimum isn’t necessarily the budget you should use.

There’s a difference between:

  • What the platform allows you to spend
  • and what gives your business enough room to learn.

That’s where campaign planning becomes important.

How much does a ChatGPT click cost?

For Click campaigns, OpenAI currently recommends starting with a maximum CPC bid of $3–$5 USD per click.

Notice the word maximum.

That doesn’t mean every click will cost $3 or $5. Your actual cost can vary depending on the auction and delivery. ChatGPT Ads uses a relevance-weighted, second-price auction to select among eligible ads.

So businesses shouldn’t think of $3–$5 as a guaranteed price per click. Think of it as a starting bid range recommended by OpenAI for CPC campaigns.

What is CPC in ChatGPT Ads?

CPC = Cost Per Click.

With a Clicks campaign, you’re buying traffic. You are charged for valid clicks, not simply for every impression your ad receives.

For example: if your campaign generates 100 valid clicks and your average CPC is $3, your media spend would be approximately $300. Your actual CPC can be higher or lower depending on campaign delivery and auction dynamics.

What is CPM?

CPM = Cost Per 1,000 Impressions.

If your goal is visibility rather than clicks, you can choose the Views objective. With Views campaigns, you’re billed based on impressions served.

For example: a $20 CPM means approximately $20 for every 1,000 impressions. This is useful when your objective is reach and exposure, rather than getting users to click through immediately.

What are oCPC and oCPM?

This is where things can get confusing.

For Conversions campaigns, eligible advertisers can choose between two billing methods:

  • oCPC — Optimized Cost Per Click. The campaign is optimized toward conversions, but you are billed per valid click.
  • oCPM — Optimized Cost Per Mille. The campaign is optimized toward conversions, but you are billed per 1,000 impressions.

The important point: you do not pay per conversion. The campaign is optimized toward your selected conversion event, while billing happens through clicks or impressions depending on the option selected.

So which objective should a business choose?

That depends on what you’re trying to accomplish.

If your goal is…Consider
Build visibilityViews / CPM
Drive website trafficClicks / CPC
Generate a measurable business actionConversions / oCPC or oCPM

Don’t choose an objective simply because it sounds more advanced. Choose the objective that matches the business outcome you’re trying to measure.

Can I control my ChatGPT Ads budget?

Yes. Ads Manager currently lets advertisers choose between:

  • Daily budget
  • Campaign-total budget

A daily budget represents an average amount over a seven-day period. It isn’t necessarily a hard same-day spending ceiling. OpenAI says daily spend can be higher or lower on individual days, with total spend limited to seven times the selected daily budget over the applicable seven-day period.

For businesses testing the platform for the first time, OpenAI recommends starting with a daily budget so you can monitor delivery and performance before committing to a campaign-total budget.

How much should I budget for my first ChatGPT Ads campaign?

This is probably the question businesses care about most. And there isn’t a universal answer.

Your starting budget should depend on:

  • Your campaign objective
  • Your target market
  • Your product or service
  • Your expected customer value
  • Your landing page
  • Your conversion event
  • How much data you need to make a decision

A sensible first campaign is usually about learning, not trying to scale immediately. You want enough budget to answer questions like:

  • Are we reaching the right conversations?
  • Are people clicking?
  • Is the traffic relevant?
  • Are users converting?
  • Which messaging is working?

Once you have those answers, you have something useful to optimize.

Does ChatGPT Ads have a standard CPC benchmark?

Not really — and businesses should be careful with anyone giving them a guaranteed benchmark.

OpenAI currently says ChatGPT Ads is still in beta and does not yet have performance benchmarks across advertisers, industries or campaign types. Results can vary based on campaign objective, creative, landing page experience, relevance and overall setup.

That means: don’t build your business case around someone else’s promised CPC or conversion rate. Your own campaign data matters more.

What determines whether my ad gets shown?

This is another important difference in how businesses should think about ChatGPT Ads. It’s not simply: Keyword → Bid → Ad.

OpenAI says its ad system considers multiple signals, including:

  • The context and intent of the current conversation
  • Landing page
  • Ad title and copy
  • Advertiser-provided context hints
  • Select signals from the broader ChatGPT experience when ads personalization is enabled

This means the context in which your product is relevant matters.

What are “context hints”?

Context hints are additional information advertisers provide at the ad-group level to help OpenAI understand when their product or service may be relevant. They can describe:

  • What — product features, services, pricing or availability
  • Who — customer needs or circumstances
  • When — situations or decisions where the offering may be useful.

But there’s an important distinction: context hints are not traditional keywords. They don’t guarantee that your ad will appear for a specific conversation or audience. They’re there to help the system understand relevance.

Who actually sees ChatGPT Ads?

Not every ChatGPT user sees ads. OpenAI currently says ads are not shown to users on Plus, Pro or Business plans, or to accounts identified as belonging to users under 18.

That’s an important consideration when estimating the audience available to your campaign.

Can I target different countries?

Yes, country targeting is part of campaign setup, although availability and targeting capabilities depend on the account and market.

One important detail for new self-serve advertisers: some accounts may initially be restricted to advertising in their home country. OpenAI says international targeting becomes available after identity verification and reaching the required advertising spend in the home country.

So if your business operates internationally, don’t assume every market will immediately be available in your first campaign.

What should I measure?

Don’t stop at: “How many clicks did we get?”

Ads Manager currently provides reporting for: Impressions, Clicks, Spend, CTR, Average CPC, Average CPM, Conversions.

For a business campaign, the more useful question is: did the campaign produce the business outcome we were trying to achieve? For example:

  • Visibility → Did relevant users see us?
  • Traffic → Did they click?
  • Engagement → Did they interact with the site?
  • Conversion → Did they complete the action we care about?

That is the difference between reporting campaign activity and measuring business performance.

So, what should your first ChatGPT Ads campaign look like?

Don’t start with: “How much money should we put into ChatGPT Ads?”

Start with:

  1. Who are we trying to reach? Define the customer, not just the demographic.
  2. What problem are they trying to solve? Think about the situations and decisions that lead them to your product.
  3. Where is our product genuinely relevant? This becomes important when developing your context strategy.
  4. What do we want users to do? Choose the campaign objective around the business outcome.
  5. What can we afford to learn? Set a test budget that gives you enough room to gather useful data.
  6. What will make us increase, change or stop the campaign? Decide this before the campaign starts.

The bigger question isn’t “How much does ChatGPT Ads cost?”

It’s: “What does it cost us to learn whether ChatGPT can become a meaningful advertising channel for our business?”

That’s a much better question.

Because the first campaign shouldn’t just answer how much you spent. It should help you understand which audiences matter, which conversations matter, which messages resonate, which campaigns convert — and whether ChatGPT deserves a bigger place in your advertising strategy.

ChatGPT Ads is still evolving, and OpenAI says the platform’s buying, delivery, measurement and optimization capabilities will continue to change during the beta.

So don’t approach your first campaign as a finished playbook. Approach it as a structured test with a business question behind it.
