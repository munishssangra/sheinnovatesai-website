The Process

How We Approach AI Search Visibility

We don't start with a service. We start with evidence. Every engagement begins with understanding how AI currently sees your brand, where the gaps are, and what your buyers may actually be asking. Our service layer is backed by our AI Search Visibility Intelligence Platform — a proprietary framework developed by researcher Tandeep Sangra from real client problems we've encountered while working in this emerging space.

01
Establish Your AI Visibility Baseline
Start with what AI can see today.

We begin with our Free AI Visibility Check, scanning your website across five foundational areas: AI crawlability · Schema · Entity authority · Trust signals · Content readability.

This gives us an initial picture of where your biggest AI Search gaps may exist. No assumptions. No generic checklist. A starting point based on your actual website.

02
Find the Questions & Visibility Gaps
Being technically visible doesn't mean being recommended.

We look beyond the website to understand:

  • What questions your buyers are likely asking AI
  • Whether your brand appears in those answers
  • How competitors are being discovered and cited
  • Which sources AI relies on
  • Where your brand is missing, misunderstood or underrepresented
As our platform evolves into MVP2, Persona Intelligence will help identify realistic buyer questions rather than relying only on manually selected keywords.

This is where we move from “Is my website AI-ready?” to “Is my brand visible when my buyers ask AI?”

03
Measure, Diagnose & Prioritize
We don't just show you a score. We tell you what the gap means.

Where possible, we track your brand across AI search environments and examine: Visibility → Mentions → Recommendations → Citations → Competitor presence.

Then we translate those findings into practical priorities: What is missing? Why might it matter? What should you fix first?

MVP2 is being built around continuous citation tracking, competitor comparison and plain-language diagnosis rather than simply giving clients another dashboard of numbers.
04
Fix What the Evidence Tells Us
Only now do we recommend the right service.

Depending on what we uncover, that may mean: AEO · GEO · SEO for AI Search Engines · Entity Optimization · Content Optimization · Structured Data · Technical SEO · AI Crawlability · Citation & Source Signals.

We don't recommend every service to every client. The gaps determine the work.

As our platform develops, Smart Fix Suggestions will help surface potential schema and page-level improvements, with human expert review before implementation.
05
Monitor. Learn. Improve.
AI visibility isn't a one-time optimization.

AI answers change. Buyer questions change. Competitors change. Sources change. So the process doesn't end when an implementation is complete. We continue to look at:

  • Citation trends
  • Brand mentions
  • Competitor movement
  • New buyer-query opportunities
  • Changes in AI responses
  • Emerging visibility gaps

The goal is not simply to “optimize once.” It's to build a system for continuous AI Search Visibility improvement.
