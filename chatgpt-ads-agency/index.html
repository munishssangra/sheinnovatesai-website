What This Actually Is

Advertising Inside a Conversation, Not a Results Page

ChatGPT Ads let a business appear below a ChatGPT response, while someone is actively exploring, comparing, or deciding — a fundamentally different placement than a search results page or a social feed.

What the ad looks like

As per OpenAI's own format spec, each ad includes an advertiser name, favicon, headline, description copy, a landing page link, and an image — shown clearly labeled below the AI's answer, never inside it.

What decides if your ad shows

OpenAI's system weighs the context and intent of the live conversation, your landing page, your ad copy, and the context hints you provide — plus personalization signals, if enabled.

How Pricing Actually Works

Views, Clicks, or Conversions — Priced Differently

ChatGPT Ads runs on impression and valid-click billing, decided by which objective you choose. Here's what OpenAI's own documentation says about each.

Views (CPM)

Billed per 1,000 impressions served — best when the goal is visibility and exposure rather than a direct action.

Clicks (CPC)

Billed per valid click. OpenAI's own guidance recommends starting with a maximum CPC bid of $3–$5 USD, using their bid-strength indicator to check if that bid is competitive.

Conversions (oCPC / oCPM)

Optimized toward a conversion event, but billed on valid clicks or impressions — not per conversion itself, per OpenAI's documentation.

Daily budget minimum

The standard US minimum is $25/day, representing an average spend target over a seven-day period rather than a fixed daily ceiling.

Why This Isn't Google Ads

Context Hints Aren't Keywords

This is the single biggest mindset shift we walk clients through. Get this wrong, and a campaign built on keyword habits underperforms from day one.

What OpenAI actually says

Context hints are set at the ad-group level and describe conversations, topics, or keywords where your product may be relevant. OpenAI is explicit: they are not exact-match keywords, and they don't guarantee delivery in any specific conversation.

What that means for strategy

Instead of bidding on "AI SEO," we build hints around the actual conversation someone would be having — "why isn't ChatGPT recommending my company" reflects real intent far better than a category label ever could.

Before You Start

Prerequisites Suggested by the She Innovates AI Team

These are the exact questions clients ask us in the first call. Getting them settled upfront saves a week of back-and-forth once campaigns are ready to launch.

Who should actually create the account?

You should — using a company email address, not ours. Per OpenAI's own account-setup process, the advertiser account is tied to whoever creates it, and treating it as a long-term brand asset (not something built on an agency team member's personal login) protects you if you ever change agencies.

How do we actually get access as your agency?

Once your account exists, you invite us directly — Settings, then Users, then Invite — using our team email. No credential sharing, no logging in as you. Access is granted and can be revoked by you at any time.

Admin, Member, or Viewer — which role do we need?

OpenAI's Ads Manager supports three ad-account roles: Ad Account Admin, Ad Account Member, and Ad Account Viewer. We typically ask for Member access — enough to build and manage campaigns — rather than Admin, which also controls billing and user permissions on the account. You stay in control of who else has access.

Whose card is on file for billing?

Yours. A valid billing profile and payment method need to be set up on your account before any campaign can go live — OpenAI's own troubleshooting guidance flags this as the first thing to check if ads aren't serving. We never bill through our own account on your behalf.

Do we need a finished landing page before we start?

Not finished — but real. Your landing page is one of the signals OpenAI's own system weighs when deciding whether to show your ad, so a generic homepage link performs worse than even a simple page built around the specific conversation you're targeting.

What if we're not sure about our budget yet?

Come with a rough range, not a fixed number. The platform's own minimum is $25/day in the US, so anything below that isn't workable — beyond that floor, we'll help you size a sensible first test budget together rather than asking you to commit upfront.
